In its original conception I set out to explore the banality of the judge, marrying the actions of the god Anubis with those of an exhausted bureaucrat. You can see some influence from my reading of Fernando Pessoa's The Book of Disquiet, as well as a bit of Camus's The Rebel. It's an old piece, and apart from ten small copyedits I have left it raw.

The essay never names Anubis, who weighs the heart against a feather, so the film gives him one object outside the office: a brass balance with a small black jackal on its pillar. It appears three times, the last after hours, a feather set against a single permit. The more abstract lines are shot as evidence photographs, a ring and keys, newborn bracelets and a dog tag lying on manila forms under the same fluorescent light. This was the first film out of the pipeline, and I hope the flawed shots and evident sloppiness serve as an example of the importance of the human touch, at least in directing AI.
