Gardening is the third film I've made with Scriptorium, and the longest by a wide margin. My first pass generated every shot straight from the text, and I threw it out when rooms kept changing from one shot to the next and the same few faces turned up on different characters. For the rebuild I made each location as a clay set from real 3D models and placed every character in it as a mannequin tinted to match their clothes. Painting the stills over those sets kept the diner, the lab and the house the same from shot to shot. I recorded the narration myself after a light edit of the text. In the last section the narrator loses his hold on events, and the film flickers into a photographic negative and stays there. Plenty of shots are still rough: faces drift between cuts and hands sink into the props.
