The film ends in negative. Over the last stretch the narrator's grip on what is happening goes, and the picture goes with him, flickering into a photographic negative over several lines before it stays there. The first board, generated straight from the text, had no sense of place and kept reusing the same faces, so I scrapped it and built each location as a clay set from real 3D models. Every person stands in that set as a mannequin tinted to their clothes, and the stills are painted over the top, so people stay where they were in the shot before. I narrated it myself after a light edit so the text would read aloud. Gardening is the third film and the one the first two were practice for. A lot of it is rough, faces drift and hands sink into props, and I redid shots until the time and money ran out.
