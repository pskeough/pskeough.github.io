## Architecture

A massive, automated generalist novel rewriting pipeline designed to take any text and "explode" it into rich, 18+ adult, dark fantasy prose. Currently a CLI pipeline; designed to connect to a frontend GUI for easy entry and management.

## How It Works

1. Extracts a **narrative skeleton** (plot beats) from the original text.
2. Dynamically updates a **character psychological roster** as the rewrite proceeds.
3. Uses a highly specific system prompt enforcing visceral, sensory horror and mythic lyricism.
4. Enforces a strict minimum **1.5× word-count expansion**, slowing pacing dramatically and preventing the model from summarizing or eliding scenes.
