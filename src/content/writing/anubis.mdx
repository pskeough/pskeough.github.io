## About the Work

A clerk narrates a working day at the permit window: nine hours, four ink pads, approvals and refusals for strangers he never meets. Anubis weighed hearts against a feather. The clerk weighs lives by quota and cannot tell what his own judgment weighs. Written years ago under the influence of Pessoa's *The Book of Disquiet* and Camus's *The Rebel*, and left raw on purpose. It was later made into a narrated short film.
