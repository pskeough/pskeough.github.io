## About the Work

A narrator becomes intensely, almost parasitically infatuated with an unseen man by the ocean. The piece details the narrator's struggle between shame, isolation, and a self-destructive urge to surrender to the sea — exploring the agonizing vulnerability of desire and the internal conflict between the safety of isolation (the *worm* in the tunnels) and the painful exposure of conscious connection.
