## About the Work

A fable-like tale of a farming tribe living in servitude to a tyrannical, sentient "amber sun." When a boy dares to look directly at the sun, it turns red and inflicts famine. To appease it, the tribe brutally sacrifices the boy, inadvertently birthing a new, cursed caste known as **the maddened**.

An allegory for dogmatic adherence to abusive structures and the creation of scapegoats — the tension between blind obedience for survival and the crushing weight of systemic cosmic tyranny.
