## About the Work

A grim reflection from a doomed sailor as his ship slowly succumbs to the sea. He recounts the psychological decay of his crewmates and the encroaching certainty of drowning — a meditation on the inevitability of death, the fragile illusion of security, and the sublime, indifferent power of the ocean.
