## About the Work

This piece was written a number of years ago as an exploration of an unreliable narrator alongside light psychological horror. A lot of the imagery and focus was inspired by my earlier years as a mycology enthusiast, with the Eden workshop clearly borrowing from agar and mycelium cultivation. I wanted to create a sense of dissonance and unease with the narrator, having his perception of events be skewed, inconsistent, and volatile. Pairing this with some light body horror created a visual identity that inspired me to make this into a full-length, locally generated AI film. Check out the pipeline I used to make it [here](/projects/scriptorium/).

## Story

There are three of us sitting around the sycamore table. The condiments bear worn labels, witness to the stillness of the collective. Movement is reflected on a patron’s glass, between the collective assertion of presence lies the intricate imbalance of stance and soul. My partner, she sits to my left. Her face is painted white, her hand rests on our guest’s. It lingers there, as we are caught in this moment, she extends her desire through their connection. She brought me here with a hurried hand, I followed without question. Little can stop me from being pushed, she is equally backbone and gust. Her friend sits there, he smiles. He is uncouth, ungroomed. His scent is strong, but not unpleasant. Like tobacco, only light, distant. Black ink covers his exposed wrist, it spirals upward into his overcoat. It was raining on the way here, perhaps that is what has dulled his scent.

“Have you brought it?” My partner asks, her extended hand lingering on his.

“I have it,” he says, pulling back from her embrace. In his jacket’s inner pocket there is a brown bag, it’s crumpled around some smaller container within it. I know what it contains. Its unexposed properties torment me, but I say nothing. It’s not my place to, I was told not to speak to the man. I follow orders like an aged soldier, marching forward to wherever my commander wishes.
