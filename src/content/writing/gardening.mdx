## About the Work

A surreal, clinical narrative detailing a couple's descent into an occult obsession. They purchase mysterious "seeds" that feed on human blood, cultivating them in a sterile laboratory setting. The process is depicted with detached, scientific precision, masking the underlying madness.

The piece explores themes of fatalism, the corruption of the maternal/creation instinct, and transhumanism achieved through self-destruction — the characters seek a twisted divinity ("Eden") by sacrificing their own humanity and flesh to their horrific crop.
