## About the Work

A high-stakes, 40-day chess match between two ancient, synthetic entities. The victor claims a prized ruby ring and the life of his opponent. The true horror unfolds in the aftermath: the winner's children inadvertently consume poisoned blood from a barrel during their victory feast, leading to a frenzied, orgiastic mass suicide and slaughter.

A bleak examination of ambition, legacy, and the cyclical nature of violence — the narrative suggests that ultimate victory and power (symbolized by the ring regaining its crimson hue) demand absolute, horrific sacrifice, rendering the concepts of family and triumph meaningless.
