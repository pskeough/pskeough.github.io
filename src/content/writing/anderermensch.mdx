## About the Work

Set in a scorched future where a malignant sun has driven humanity into subterranean colonies, the story follows a young scavenger named **Lepro**. After suffering a debilitating injury on the ruined surface, he faces a slow death on the dunes. He is rescued by enigmatic, "angelic" entities — seemingly perfect, immortal beings composed of light and flawless flesh — who lead him into a surreal underground complex for a series of metaphysical trials.

The narrative explores the tension between mortal fragility and sterile divinity. The angels offer Lepro a perfected, immortal existence devoid of suffering. Lepro recognizes the horrific, empty gluttony inherent in this "perfection." He asserts his will, rejects the divine salvation, and chooses instead to embrace his flawed, terminal humanity — an *Anderermensch*.
