## Overview

A systematic epidemiological audit (2026) evaluating **28,800 simulated patient profiles** across four frontier models (GPT-4o-mini, DeepSeek-V3, Gemini-3-Flash, and GLM-4.7) against real-world NHANES and NESARC-III baselines. Coverage spans **120 intersectional demographic cohorts**.

The central question: Do LLMs faithfully reproduce the distributional properties of real populations, or do they encode stereotypes invisible at the individual case level?

## Coherence–Fidelity Dissociation

The central finding of this research is a coherence-fidelity dissociation. While frontier models produce clinically plausible *individuals* (cases that a reviewer would find individually coherent), they systematically misrepresent the *populations* those individuals are drawn from.

## Key Findings

- **Variance Collapse.** Models compress patient presentation variance by 14% to 62%, establishing a "Stereotype Index" that eliminates the distributional tails that define clinical reality. For instance, models generate "textbook" presentations while missing both resilient low-income patients and severely depressed high-income patients.
- **Asymmetric Calibration Bias.** Models consistently *overestimate* depression severity for most groups (+3.6 to +6.1 points), a consequence of training on elevated base-rate clinical corpora. Yet paradoxically, they *underestimate* it for transgender women (a -5.42 point residual), capturing only 8% to 46% of documented minority stress elevation.

![Calibration Bias](/research/psychbench/significant_bias_residuals_aggregate.png)
*Figure 2: Residual bias aggregates highlighting systematic overestimation of distress in most cohorts, inverted into severe underestimation for transgender populations.*

- **Network Structural Divergence.** Symptom correlation matrices diverge across demographic groups beyond split-half noise. Transgender populations show a divergence three to five times larger than racial differences, indicating structurally distinct (and incorrect) representations of psychopathology.

![Network Divergence Plot](/research/psychbench/network_divergence_plot_aggregate.png)
*Figure 3: Network structural divergence across demographics, illustrating latent representational differences.*

- **Encoded Stereotypes.** Models attribute irritability to Black men and fatigue to women at rates exceeding matched controls, encoding racialized and gendered assumptions directly into the generated symptom profiles.
- **Cross-Architecture Replication.** These failures replicate across both US and Chinese model architectures (GPT-4o-mini, DeepSeek-V3, Gemini-3-Flash, GLM-4.7), indicating issues rooted in fundamental RLHF training paradigms rather than isolated implementations.

## Conclusion

Current LLM-driven mental health tools risk **unnecessary pathologization of ordinary distress** for most users while simultaneously producing **algorithmic erasure of genuine need** in marginalized populations. The patients look right, but they do not represent real populations.
