## Overview

Can a research group answer questions over its own documents with a small model on its own hardware, or does it need a hosted frontier API? The study tests three decisions such a group makes: whether to use retrieval, whether to fine-tune, and whether a local model can judge the answers.

The primary corpus is 26 published papers from Robert Tai's research group, standing in for a collection that cannot leave the group, with QASPER and COVID-QA as public replications. Three local models (MiniCPM 1B, Phi-4-mini 3.8B and Llama-3.1-8B) run on one 16 GB consumer GPU and are compared against GPT-5.1, Claude Sonnet 4.6 and DeepSeek-v4-pro under a matched-evidence condition, in which the hosted models receive the same retrieved passages as the local ones. A hosted judge, Gemini 3 Flash, compares the answers blind in both presentation orders, alongside three local Gemma 4 judges.

## Current results

- **Retrieval is the largest lever.** Across all three collections it improves every local model. On top of retrieval, fine-tuning adds a small gain that survives correction for multiple tests in 2 of 9 model-by-corpus cells.
- **With the same passages, the hosted models still lead by a small amount.** On the private corpus most pairs end in a tie, and when the judge picks a winner it is nearly always the hosted answer. Net preference, the share of pairs the judge gives the hosted answer minus the share it gives the local one, is +0.14 over the 3.8B and +0.24 over the 8B, while the 1B trails far behind. For the 3.8B the gap is smaller than for the 8B. Each size is a model from a different developer, and the results describe these three models.
- **Expert gold points the same way.** On 38 private items with answers written by Robert Tai, GPT-5.1 recovers more of the expert answer than the local 8B given the same passages.
- **Local judges agree on direction and read a larger gap.** In all nine private comparisons the Gemma 4 judges reach the hosted judge's verdict on which side leads, and they rank the models the same way. Under the 12B and E4B judges the hosted lead is larger than under the hosted judge. Which judge is closer to the truth is still open.
